# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Finance staff, analysts, and administrators who review reports, adjust report structure, upload source data, and manage access in a single dashboard.

## Product Purpose

Carmen Financial BI Hub is an internal reporting workspace for viewing financial reports, configuring report definitions, loading GL and budget data, managing access, and exporting or printing the final reports.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, trustworthy. The interface should feel efficient and controlled, with enough visual polish to read as a serious finance tool without becoming heavy or noisy.

## Anti-references

- Loud SaaS marketing chrome
- Over-decorated dashboards
- Soft pastel admin templates
- Cramped forms with unclear actions
- Generic purple-first app shells

## Design Principles

- Keep the report workflow obvious and low-friction.
- Make primary actions easy to find and hard to mistake.
- Use spacing and contrast to separate system states.
- Preserve business logic and surface it with a cleaner presentation.
- Treat errors, access rules, and import states as first-class UI.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for strong contrast, keyboard-friendly controls, clear validation states, and readable layouts at common desktop and tablet widths. Keep motion restrained and avoid relying on color alone to communicate state.
